Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vedlikeholdt collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you use our website and service.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Vedlikeholdt. Before publishing this policy, the operator should replace this description with the correct legal name, organisation number, postal address, and contact details.

Privacy questions and requests can be sent to support@vedlikeholdt.no.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, name, phone number, authentication identifiers, and account settings.
  • Property and vehicle information: names, addresses, registration details, maintenance records, plans, and related metadata that you enter.
  • Uploaded content: photos, receipts, documents, floorplans, files, captions, extracted text, and other information you choose to store.
  • Preferences and activity: notification settings, language preference, project information, and interactions needed to provide the service.
  • Technical information: authentication session data, browser/device information, IP address, timestamps, and security or error logs that may be generated by the website or hosting providers.

3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • create and secure your account;
  • provide, sync, and maintain the service across devices;
  • store and display your homes, vehicles, photos, documents, and floorplans;
  • send service-related emails, verification messages, and account notices;
  • provide family, sharing, and access-control features that you request;
  • detect misuse, protect users, troubleshoot errors, and improve reliability;
  • comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

Depending on the situation, the legal basis may be performance of a contract, consent, legitimate interests in security and service operation, or compliance with a legal obligation. We do not sell personal information.

4. Service providers

We use service providers to operate Vedlikeholdt. They may process information only as needed to provide their services or as otherwise permitted by law:

  • Supabase provides authentication, database hosting, and storage for account data and uploaded content.
  • Vercel may provide website hosting, deployment, logs, and content delivery.
  • domene.no may provide domain registration and DNS services.
  • GitHub provides source-code hosting and version control. User content is not intentionally stored in the source repository.
  • Google Fonts and other libraries or content-delivery services may process technical request information when their resources are loaded.

Before launch, the operator should confirm each provider's data-processing terms, subprocessors, storage regions, and international-transfer safeguards. Where required, appropriate data-processing agreements and standard contractual safeguards should be in place.

5. Storage and security

Account records and uploaded content are stored using authenticated Supabase services with access controls intended to prevent users from accessing one another's data. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including authentication, access policies, and encrypted connections.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. You should use a strong, unique password and avoid uploading information that you are not authorised to store.

6. Cookies and browser storage

The service uses essential browser storage for authentication sessions, language and interface preferences, notifications, and local caches that help the application work. These technologies are used to provide the service and are not intended for advertising tracking. Clearing browser storage may sign you out or remove local cached preferences; cloud data remains subject to your account and retention rules.

7. Sharing and disclosures

We do not sell your information. We may disclose information to the service providers listed above, to people you deliberately invite or share with, when required by law, or when necessary to protect the service, users, or legal rights. We will not make your private account content public by default.

8. Retention and deletion

We retain account and content data while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. Some information may remain temporarily in backups, logs, or provider systems after deletion before it is overwritten or securely deleted. We may retain limited information when required by law or needed to resolve disputes and enforce agreements.

You can request account or content deletion by contacting support@vedlikeholdt.no. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of processing of your personal information. You may also have rights to data portability and to object to certain processing, or to withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis.

We aim to respond within the deadlines required by applicable law. You may also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet, or the relevant authority where you live or work.

10. Children

The service is not directed at children who are not legally able to consent to data processing. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

11. International processing

Our providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable, we will use legally recognised transfer mechanisms and safeguards. The operator should keep the provider and transfer documentation current as the service configuration changes.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our service, providers, or legal obligations change. The latest version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will be communicated where required.
